About Ochello

Welcome to Ochello, a sanctuary for art lovers and creators alike. Our gallery is more than just a space to display art; it's a community, a conversation, and a celebration of human expression.

Our History

Ochello was founded in 1985 by art enthusiast Eleanor Vance. What started as a small collection in her home quickly grew into a renowned gallery showcasing both emerging and established artists. Eleanor's vision was to create a space where art was accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or knowledge.

Over the years, Ochello has hosted countless exhibitions, workshops, and events, becoming a vital part of the local art scene. We are proud to continue Eleanor's legacy of promoting creativity and fostering a love for art in the community.
