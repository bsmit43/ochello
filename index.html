About Ochello Art Galleries

Welcome to Ochello, a sanctuary for art enthusiasts and a platform for emerging and established artists alike. Founded in 2008 by art collector and visionary, Eleanor Vance, Ochello Art Galleries has become a cornerstone of the contemporary art scene.

Our mission is to cultivate a deeper appreciation for art by showcasing diverse perspectives and fostering meaningful dialogues. We believe that art has the power to transform, inspire, and connect us all.

Ochello features two distinct gallery spaces: The Main Gallery, which hosts large-scale exhibitions of internationally renowned artists, and The Annex, a dedicated space for showcasing the work of emerging talent. We also host regular artist talks, workshops, and community events.

Our team of passionate art professionals is dedicated to providing exceptional service and creating a welcoming environment for all visitors. We invite you to explore our collection, engage with our artists, and discover the transformative power of art.
